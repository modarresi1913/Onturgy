# KERNEL.md

The Onturgic Kernel. Six dimensions, derived from an explicit criterion, with exclusions stated.

## Criterion

> *What must an architecture make explicit if it is to remain revisable by those it affects?*

## The six dimensions

| Dimension | Question |
|---|---|
| **Agency** | Who can initiate, intervene, delegate, refuse, or override? |
| **Value** | *For declared-objective architectures:* does the operational objective diverge from the declared? For undeclared architectures, this row reduces to Authority + Contestability. |
| **Authority** | Who holds final decision power, and over what? |
| **Memory** | What persists, what is forgotten, and who controls continuity? |
| **Exit** | Can participants leave without prohibitive architectural barriers? |
| **Contestability** | Can rules and decisions be challenged through a procedure that can change them? |

## Status of the six

They are a *design proposal* justified by the criterion, not a metaphysical discovery. They do not exhaust human existence. Their necessity and sufficiency are an empirical question (kill criterion 6, see [CHANGELOG.md](./CHANGELOG.md)).

## Value as a conditional dimension (v2 revision)

The first edition framed Value as a primitive of every architecture. The four-pass empirical program (see [results/](./results/)) found that Value does unique audit work only for architectures that **declare an objective whose operational behavior diverges from that declared objective**. For architectures without declared objectives, and for declared architectures where the failure is implementation rather than value-divergence, Value reduces to Authority (who set the objective) and Contestability (could the objective be challenged).

**Refined framing:** Value is primitive of architectures that declare an objective AND whose operational behavior diverges from that declared objective. For other architectures, the audit reduces to five dimensions without loss.

This refinement is itself a candidate for further empirical test. The falsification frontier (Pass IV) names three patterns that would falsify it; none have been observed on the N=50 corpus.

## Three layers

1. **Kernel:** the six dimensions above.
2. **Capacity layer:** *Materiality* (who owns infrastructure, who can build, who can block, who pays, who maintains) and *Labor* (who performs the hidden work). Nothing is constructed from ideas alone.
3. **Evaluation layer:** *Legitimacy* (why is this architecture entitled to constrain alternatives, measured against the alternatives actually available) and *Reconstructability*. These are judgments made with the kernel's evidence, not additional descriptive dimensions.

## What the kernel is NOT neutral about

Exit and Contestability are values. A distribution that denies those below it any exit or any route to challenge its rules is excluded by the kernel. The kernel is a minimal constitutional-liberal floor, not a value-free substrate. This is where the analogy to a software kernel breaks. It is stated openly and exposed to test.

## Fields outside the kernel

Body, care, justice, solidarity, ecology, culture, emotion, time, truth, identity, nonhuman agency, mortality, meaning remain outside the kernel. Putting them inside would turn it into an encyclopedia of preferred values.

## Reconstructability is not a seventh dimension

It is the target property: the degree to which the six, with the material conditions below, allow affected parties to build a meaningfully different version. It is measured through the possibility graph (see §3 of the paper).

## Self-hosting (§11)

The kernel is governed by the principles it imposes. It is versioned, publicly revisable, and tested by the same method as everything else. See [CHANGELOG.md](./CHANGELOG.md) for the history of revisions with origin tags.
