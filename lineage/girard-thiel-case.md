# Case: Girard–Thiel Lineage

> The illustrative case from §9.2 of the paper.

## The relationship

René Girard was a French-born literary critic and philosopher at Stanford. Peter Thiel studied under Girard at Stanford, has publicly credited him as an influence on his career, and has taken part in events honoring him. This is a documented lineage edge.

## What is documented

- **Self-report:** Thiel has publicly credited Girard as an influence in interviews, talks, and writings.
- **Documentary:** Stanford records show Thiel studied under Girard.
- **Behavioral:** Thiel's investment and writing decisions show patterns consistent with applying Girard's framework to domains Girard himself wrote little about.

The grade of evidence is mixed: largely self-report and public statement for the link itself, with behavioral evidence for the application.

## The conceptual gap

Girard wrote almost nothing about technology. Thiel's technology investments (PayPal, Facebook, Palantir) and his writings (Zero to One) apply a Girardian framework — mimetic desire, scapegoating, the violence of the crowd — to a domain its author left largely untouched.

If the lineage is real, it ran through **reinterpretation**: a builder applied a framework to a domain its author did not address.

## Three cautions (per §9.2)

1. **Selection bias:** This is one prominent case. Prominent cases select for stories already told. The case illustrates the mechanism; it is not evidence for it.
2. **Evidence grade:** The documented link is largely self-report and public statement. The Onturgic claim is not that Girard caused Thiel's choices mechanically, but that a conceptual framework can become architectural through the person who learns to see through it.
3. **No unsourced lineage edges:** Any further list of influences on a living person requires a source for each edge. An unsourced "intellectual profile" is exactly the kind of untestable lineage claim the graph is designed to prevent.

## Why this case is in the kernel paper

The case illustrates three things the lineage graph demands:
- Edges must be documented with evidence (self-report, documentary, behavioral).
- The grade of evidence is recorded for every edge.
- Reinterpretation is a valid lineage edge, distinct from faithful transmission.

## The testable claim (§9.3)

> **Lineage claim:** differences in documented lineage predict differences in the architectures builders produce, beyond what field, market, and resource constraints explain.

It can be tested by comparing architectures across builders with different documented lineages while controlling for those constraints. It can fail (§16, kill criterion 8).
